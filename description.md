## User Request

The following changes are requested for evaluation on the prototype. Some items may need clarification — the user is open to questions if anything is unclear.

1. **OTP during Registration**: An OTP should be shared with the Patient during registration, tied to their phone number (via WhatsApp or SMS).

2. **Treatment Plan Approval — Logging Only**: The Ahilney operation team should NOT approve or reject the treatment plan provided by the doctor — it is for logging purposes only.

3. **Booking with RS — Two Options**: When booking with an RS, the patient has two options:
   - Reserve an online consultation with one of Ahilney's doctors (online).
   - Upload a treatment plan if they have one from a doctor outside the Ahilney network.
   The uploaded document is optional.

4. **Patient Responsibility for Uploaded Document**: The patient is responsible for the document they upload. If the uploaded treatment plan is wrong, the patient should not be able to request a refund. The patient must approve the terms and conditions before booking with the RS.

5. **Cancel Home Care Appointment**: During Home Care with an RS, the patient should have the option to cancel the appointment.

6. **Refund & Session Management — Operation Team Only**: Only the Operation Team should have the option to issue a refund or add sessions to a package purchased by the patient.

7. **RS Region Selection**: The RS should define the regions they will work in. This should be implemented as a multi-select dropdown, or optionally an interactive map that lets the RS select regions inside Egypt relative to their location.

8. **Patient Location Permission**: The app should request the patient's location and handle the case where the patient refuses to grant location permission.

9. **RS Sorting by Proximity**: During home care selection, the app should sort RS providers based on proximity to the patient's location (nearest first).

10. **RS Accept/Reject Booking**: RS providers should be able to accept or reject booking requests made by patients.
