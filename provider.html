How to test the Provider App

Login Provider Authentication

Switch providers in the dropdown above. The phone resets to the login screen. Use the pre-filled credentials (or any RS email + password "password") to log in.

Requests Accept or Reject Homecare Bookings

Tap "New Patient Booking" above to inject a pending request. Then on the Home tab, review the request details and tap Accept or Reject. The patient app reflects the change instantly via localStorage.

Session Conduct a Home Visit Session

On a Confirmed visit card, tap Start Session. A clinical room opens with a live timer. Add notes, then tap End Session to open the mandatory post-session summary form.

Summary Submit Clinical Summary

Fill in Complaint, Diagnosis, and Treatment Plan fields. Submit creates a summary record with status "Pending" for the Admin to audit. Your wallet shows "Pending Audit" for the session fee.

Sync Real-time Cross-Tab Sync

Open admin.html in a second tab. When the admin approves your session summary, your Wallet tab immediately shows the payout as credited — no refresh needed.
