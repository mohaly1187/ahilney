How to test the Patient App

Login OTP Verification

Switch patients in the dropdown above. The phone resets to the OTP screen. Enter any 4 digits and tap Verify to log in as that patient.

Book Book a Homecare Session

Tap the Book tab → Select district → Browse RS providers → tap a card → Open profile sheet → Tap Book Now → Complete 4-step flow → Confirm. A new appointment is created with status "Waiting for confirmation".

Appointments Track Bookings & Rate Sessions

The Appointments tab shows all bookings with live status badges. Finished sessions show a Rate & Review prompt. Rejected ones show a Book Again shortcut.

Wallet View Balance & Transactions

The Wallet tab shows current balance, pending cash bookings, and full transaction history. Use the Top-Up button to add mock funds.

Profile Medical History & Treatment Plans

The Profile tab shows personal info, medical history, and approved treatment plans from doctors.
